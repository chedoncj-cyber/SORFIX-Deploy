What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites. This portal uses a minimal approach: no advertising or tracking cookies are set by us. We use browser localStorage for a single functional purpose (remembering your consent choice).

No Advertising Cookies

This portal does not use advertising cookies, analytics trackers, retargeting pixels, or social media tracking scripts. We do not share data with advertising networks.

Managing Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies will not materially affect your use of this portal, as we do not rely on cookies for core functionality.

To clear your consent preference: open your browser's developer tools, go to Application → Local Storage, and delete the cookie-consent key for this site.
