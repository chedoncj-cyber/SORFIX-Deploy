Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026  ·  Pan SORFIX

1. Data Controller

The data controller for this portal is SORFIX (operated by ChedonCJ). For privacy-related enquiries, contact: privacy@sorfix.com.

2. What Data We Collect

This is a demonstration and reference portal. We do not collect, store, or process any personal data from visitors directly through portal interactions.

No registration, login, or account creation is required or available. No forms collect personal information.

3. Server Logs

Web servers hosting this portal (provided by Render.com) may automatically capture server access logs, which can include:

  • IP addresses of visitors
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and timestamps
  • HTTP referrer

Server logs are retained for a maximum of 30 days, after which they are deleted. These logs are used solely for security monitoring and infrastructure diagnostics. They are not used for profiling or marketing.

4. Third-Party Services

This portal loads resources from third-party CDN providers. These services may process your IP address when your browser fetches resources from their servers:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com) — serves the Inter typeface. Google's privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Tailwind CSS CDN (cdn.tailwindcss.com) — serves the Tailwind utility CSS framework. Tailwind's privacy policy applies: tailwindcss.com/privacy-policy

We do not control how these third parties process data. Their privacy policies govern their respective data handling practices.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

This portal uses browser localStorage to remember your cookie consent preference. No persistent tracking cookies are set by this portal itself. See our Cookie Policy for full details.

6. Your Rights (UK GDPR)

Under UK GDPR, you have rights including: access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and to object to processing. As we do not collect or store personal data from portal interactions, most of these rights are not applicable in practice.

If you have concerns about data processed by our hosting provider's server logs, you may contact us at privacy@sorfix.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the portal following an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions: privacy@sorfix.com
