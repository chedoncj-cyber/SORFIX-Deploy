How It Works
Order Lifecycle in 4 Steps

From order submission to fill confirmation — all in under 10 milliseconds.

1

Order Received ?

REST API validates the order via Pydantic v2, pre-trade risk checks fire, and the order enters the routing engine.

📋 Summary
Think of clicking "Buy" on a stock. The system instantly checks: Is the order valid? Do you have the funds? Is the quantity within safe limits? If every check passes, the order moves into the queue. If anything fails, it's rejected right away with a clear reason — nothing moves forward until everything is verified.
2

Venue Scored ?

6-factor scoring evaluates liquidity, spread, FX cost, fee, latency and reliability across all enabled venues.

🧠 Summary
Before buying, the system scouts every available stock exchange — like comparing prices across multiple shops. Each exchange gets graded on 6 things: how much stock is available, how tight the price gap is, currency conversion cost, trading fees, how fast it responds, and how reliable it is. The highest-scoring exchanges go to the top of the list.
3

Smart Routing ?

Proportional allocation splits the order across top-scoring venues. All legs execute in parallel via FIX 5.0 SP2 / FIXT.1.1, with ApplVerID on every message.

⚡ Summary
Instead of buying all your shares from one exchange, the system splits your order across the top-ranked exchanges — like buying in bulk from multiple suppliers to get the best overall deal. All the pieces execute at the same time, so it's fast. The results are then combined into one clean purchase summary for you.
4

Fill Confirmed ?

Execution reports arrive, positions update, TCA data is stored. Fill ratio, VWAP and slippage are returned.

✅ Summary
Your purchase is done. The system collects confirmation receipts from every exchange used, calculates the average price you paid across all the splits, checks how close it got to the ideal price, and hands back a full report: how many shares were bought, at what average price, and exactly how long the whole thing took — start to finish.

Core Infrastructure & Institutional Grade Tools

From direct exchange routing to dark pool crossing — every capability an institutional desk requires, built into one system.

Core Infrastructure

6-Factor Venue Scoring ?

Liquidity 28% · Spread 18% · FX 18% · Fee 16% · Latency 10% · Reliability 10%. Weights validated at startup.

🎯 Summary
The system grades every exchange on 6 criteria before deciding where to send your order: how much stock is available (28%), how tight the buy/sell price gap is (18%), currency conversion cost (18%), trading fees (16%), response speed (10%), and uptime reliability (10%). The exchange with the highest combined score gets the most of your order.

GBM Market Data ?

Geometric Brownian Motion price simulation with 10-level order books, exponential depth decay, and 0.5bps ticks.

📊 Summary
The system generates realistic market prices using a mathematical model (GBM) — the same one used by major banks. It creates a full picture of what buyers and sellers are currently offering at each price level, with 10 layers of depth, so routing decisions are based on accurate simulated market conditions.

Proportional SOR Allocation ?

Orders split proportionally by score × capacity. Last leg takes the remainder. Up to 5-way splits supported.

🔀 Summary
Your order is divided across multiple exchanges based on how well each one scored. If Exchange A scored twice as high as Exchange B, it gets twice the shares. The final leftover always goes to the top-ranked venue. This means you automatically buy from the best places — without doing the maths yourself.

Pre-Trade Risk Engine ?

Max order qty, notional, price band, daily notional limit, and position limits checked before every order.

🛡️ Summary
Before any order is sent, the system runs a safety checklist: Is the order size within safe limits? Is the total value within your daily cap? Is the price within a reasonable range? If any check fails, the order is blocked immediately — preventing costly mistakes before they reach the market.

TWAP / VWAP Algos ?

Time-Weighted and Volume-Weighted execution algorithms with configurable duration, slices, and participation rates.

⚙️ Summary
For large orders, buying everything at once can push the price against you. These algorithms break your order into smaller slices spread over time (TWAP) or timed to match the busiest trading periods (VWAP). The result is a better average price — like buying a large amount gradually instead of all at once.

FIX 5.0 / FIXT.1.1 ?

Full FIXT.1.1 adapter per exchange. NewOrderSingle, ExecutionReport, IOI, NewOrderList, TradeCaptureReport — all FIX 5.0 SP2 message types.

🔌 Summary
FIX 5.0 SP2 (transport: FIXT.1.1) is the current institutional standard for exchange connectivity. Every message carries ApplVerID (tag 1128=9) identifying it as FIX50SP2 — enabling complex multi-leg orders, iceberg display quantity, basket NewOrderList, and IOI messages that FIX 4.2 could not express cleanly.

WebSocket Live Books ?

Stream live order book snapshots at ws://host/ws/book/{venue}/{symbol} — updated every 100ms.

📡 Summary
You can connect and watch a live stream of every buy and sell offer sitting on an exchange — like a scoreboard that refreshes 10 times per second. This shows you exactly who wants to buy or sell, at what price, and in what quantity — giving you a real-time window into the market.

Circuit Breaker ?

CLOSED/OPEN/HALF_OPEN state machine per venue. Feeds directly into reliability scoring and allocation decisions.

🔁 Summary
Like a household circuit breaker that cuts power to protect your appliances, this automatically stops sending orders to an exchange if it starts behaving erratically. Once the exchange stabilises, orders are gradually allowed through again. It prevents bad fills caused by a struggling exchange — protecting you without you having to do anything.

TCA Analytics ?

Transaction Cost Analysis endpoint with VWAP, slippage, fill ratio, and routing latency for every order.

📈 Summary
After every trade, the system produces a report card. It tells you: Did you get a good price compared to when you placed the order? How much did it cost in fees and price movement? How fast was it executed? This information helps you measure performance and continuously improve your trading strategy over time.
Institutional Trading Suite
Capabilities that close the gap with institutional networks like LSEG Autex

IOI Board ?

Indications of Interest board — brokers advertise block intent without fully committing. Buyers respond privately. Transforms routing into a trading network.

Institutional Network
Sellers post interest to buy or sell a large block; buyers respond privately. This is the founding product of Autex Trade Route — and now a core SORFIX capability. It creates a liquidity discovery layer above exchange routing.

Dark Pool Crossing ?

Internal crossing before lit exchange exposure. Midpoint price-time priority matching. Zero market impact, no exchange fees on crossed volume.

Zero Market Impact
Before sending an order to a public exchange, SORFIX checks if it can match your order against another user's opposite order internally. No exchange sees it, no price is moved. The result: better fills, lower costs, and no signal leaked to the market.

Block Trade Workflow ?

Large order negotiation — submit size, find a counterparty, agree on price before execution. Eliminates market slippage on thinly-traded names.

Negotiated Execution
For orders large enough to visibly move a price, SORFIX finds a willing counterparty and negotiates a price before touching the exchange. Both sides confirm before execution — preventing the price from running away during the trade.

Iceberg / Reserve Orders ?

Display qty visible to market; hidden reserve auto-replenishes on each fill. Prevent large orders from revealing intent and moving the price.

Hidden Depth
Like an iceberg where only the tip shows above water, only a small visible slice of your order appears on the exchange. As each slice fills, the next is automatically revealed. Other traders can't see your full order size — preventing them from front-running your position.

Basket / Portfolio Trading ?

Submit 50+ stocks with target notional weights. Each leg executes via TWAP or VWAP independently. Essential for fund rebalancing without single-name market impact.

Portfolio Rebalancing
Fund managers enter a list of stocks and target weights (e.g. "10% Apple, 8% Safaricom, 5% MTN"). SORFIX executes every leg simultaneously using smart algorithms — so the whole portfolio moves together without any one stock telegraphing the trade.

Best Execution Reporting ?

Per-order audit trail: venues considered, selection rationale, benchmark price vs achieved price. MiFID II aligned. Required by institutional desks.

Regulatory Audit Trail
For every order, SORFIX generates a complete record: which exchanges were evaluated, why the chosen ones were selected, what the reference price was, and what you actually achieved. This is the compliance report that institutional clients and regulators require — now automatic on every trade.

FIX Drop Copy ?

Real-time copies of all order flow streamed to configurable compliance endpoints. Surveillance, audit, and regulatory reporting — zero integration overhead.

Compliance Stream
Every order event — submission, acknowledgement, fill, rejection — is simultaneously copied to your compliance system in real time via FIX. Your surveillance team sees every trade as it happens, without connecting directly to the exchange or writing custom integrations.
